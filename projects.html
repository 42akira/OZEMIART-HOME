The Clock - Media and Nox

Final Animation Project from the practical seminar "Comics" 2022-2023 (school project)

Media is a figurine stuck on her minute clock hand of the city's tower clock. She is in good company of her partner Nox who has the same problem representing the hour clock hand. Every hour they meet only once for a minute until they unwillingly have to separate again.

In their endeavor to stay together they try multiple to hold on to each other. Will they succeed?
